# Business strategy — draft for first launch

Date: 14 October 2026. RM10/module, text + images + links + templates + workflow + try-it task; no video. Launch to Malaysia in Malay-first language. All figures below are planning scenarios, not sales forecasts. “Net” here means operating profit after modeled operating costs, **before income tax and founder salary**; the actual RM10k take-home target needs those included once the legal and tax setup is known.

## The offer

**Promise:** one small skill, one finished artifact. A generic “Kuasai AI” course is easy to postpone; “siapkan 10 balasan WhatsApp untuk pelanggan yang diam” has a clear finish line.

Each paid module should have: who it is for, prerequisites, expected time, third-party tool cost, outcome preview, 5–8 short steps, annotated images, direct official tool links, one editable template, one example, a task, success checklist, troubleshooting, date last checked, and the next logical module. External subscriptions and API fees must be visible before checkout.

The current [Meta Ads & AI module](https://modul-meta-ads.syahmiaof.my/) is a useful source. It is much broader than a one-result micro-module; keep it as an advanced path or split it into smaller outputs after factual review. Do not promise completion in 30 minutes for that entire guide.

## Repeat purchase: learning path, not spam

1. Ask a one-tap goal after first purchase: `Nak lebih leads`, `Nak jimat masa`, or `Nak bina project`.
2. When a learner finishes the task, show their artifact and one specific next module. Example: “Dah ada skrip WhatsApp. Seterusnya, bina FAQ supaya soalan sama tak dijawab 20 kali.”
3. Add a free progress map with three paths and locked future outcomes; the catalog remains browsable without a paywall.
4. Offer a three-module path at RM27 as an **A/B test**, with a clear RM10 standalone option. A bundle saves payment fees per module and raises average order value. Do not launch a subscription yet; content cadence and retention are unproven.
5. Send useful completion follow-up, then one relevant optional marketing recommendation with opt-out. Do not add promotional WhatsApp to a purchase confirmation by default.
6. Let members submit an artifact for peer feedback, with a moderation queue and anonymisation guidance. Publish excellent learner examples only with explicit permission.

## The self-serve automation loop

```text
Topic-specific TikTok/SEO post (tagged URL)
  → module detail + real sample page
  → account creation + RM10 checkout
  → verified payment webhook (deduplicate event)
  → grant only the purchased module
  → transactional email with direct access link
  → optional WhatsApp access notification if opted in
  → reader progress + completed artifact
  → one relevant next-module recommendation
  → optional Community invite and later marketing only by consent
  → dashboard: visit → purchase → completion → second purchase → refund/support
```

Failures need an operator queue: unpaid/pending payment gets no entitlement, webhook retries do not double-grant, email/WhatsApp delivery can retry without repeating a sale, refunds update access under a stated policy, and broken tool links create a content-maintenance task. Keep an audit trail of payment event, entitlement, consent and message status. This is the practical meaning of “duit masuk masa tidur”; a failed webhook at 3 a.m. still needs a recovery path.

## Economics of RM10

Working assumptions: Billplz Basic FPX RM1.25 per **transaction**; RM0.40–RM0.90 variable platform/support budget per order; RM1,500 monthly fixed operating costs. Payment provider and other costs need a real quote and measured support time. Refunds, discounts, tax and founder salary are excluded from this illustrative table.

| Scenario | Revenue / order | Payment + variable | Acquisition cost / order | Contribution / order | Orders/month for RM10k operating profit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| One RM10 module, organic | RM10.00 | RM1.65 | RM0 | RM8.35 | 1,378 |
| One RM10 module, RM5 paid CAC | RM10.00 | RM1.65 | RM5 | RM3.35 | 3,433 |
| Three-module path RM27, organic | RM27.00 | RM2.15 | RM0 | RM24.85 | 463 |
| Three-module path RM27, RM8 paid CAC | RM27.00 | RM2.15 | RM8 | RM16.85 | 683 |

Formula: `orders = ceil((RM10,000 + RM1,500 fixed cost) / contribution per order)`. At 463–1,378 orders/month, a small conversion rate can still require tens of thousands of qualified visits. Example only: 1,378 orders at 2% landing-to-purchase = 68,900 qualified visits/month. This is why “ambil 0.1% rakyat Malaysia” is not a usable acquisition plan. A one-time 0.1% of 34.4m residents is 34,400 people; at one RM10 purchase each that is RM344k **gross over an unspecified period**, not recurring profit.

Track gross sales, net revenue after refunds, contribution margin, new CAC, blended CAC, first-order payback, 30-day second purchase, completion, support minutes/order and true monthly operating profit. Do not scale paid ads until the first-order contribution or measured repeat value supports CAC.

## Acquisition engine

**Organic TikTok/Reels:** 3 repeatable formats per topic: (A) “kesilapan biasa” with a real screen, (B) 20-second before/after artifact, (C) one useful step from a module. Each points to a topic-specific page and UTM. AI may draft scripts, captions, subtitling, edits and variants; founder reviews facts, screenshots and final creative. Post 3–5 quality clips/week initially, then raise volume only if production is stable. No claim that posting can be fully hands-off from day one; [TikTok's public Direct Post path requires scope approval and audit](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-posting-api-get-started).

**Hooks to test:**

- Owner/PAS: “Pelanggan tanya harga, lepas tu senyap? Ini tiga balasan yang tak bunyi desperate.” Show one real example; CTA to the RM10 WhatsApp-script module.
- Student/Before–After–Bridge: “Assignment website dah siap di laptop, tapi pensyarah tak boleh buka link? Publish dalam beberapa langkah.” Show the deployed URL; CTA to the deployment module.

**Paid tests:** Start only after organic pages convert. Test three hooks per segment, one landing page each, controlled small budgets and clean attribution. Weekly decision rule: pause a creative when spend exceeds the acceptable CAC test limit without a sale; keep a winner only when enough conversions make the number useful. CTR shows attention; landing conversion and CAC decide whether it makes money. Do not use ROAS alone when bundles and repeat purchases matter.

**Other channels:** SEO pages for task phrases (“cara deploy website GitHub”, “template balas pelanggan WhatsApp”); partner with student clubs and small-business communities via genuine mini workshops or free samples; referral credit only after the purchaser completes a module. Do not promise income outcomes in ads.

## WhatsApp Community and AI support

Use one Community with an announcement group plus clear topic groups only when needed. The joining step is optional after purchase. Community rules: no selling to members, no sharing API keys/customer data, one pinned support format (`modul`, `langkah`, `screenshot tanpa rahsia`, `apa yang berlaku`). Moderate with humans at launch.

Important architecture limit: do not assume an official WhatsApp Community/group bot can read and answer every message through the Business Platform. Confirm supported API surfaces with Meta before promising that feature. Safer first version: AI FAQ assistant inside the LMS, grounded only in approved module content, with “tak pasti” + ticket escalation; a human posts a curated digest to Community. WhatsApp purchase/access notifications and marketing need separate template/consent and cost budgets.

## Six-week launch sequence

| Week | Deliverable | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 interviews, pick brand, validate three outcomes, write first modules | Repeated pain language and test-page signups |
| 2 | Design prototype: catalog, product detail, reading, library, checkout | 5 user walkthroughs; can find and complete a task on mobile |
| 3–4 | Build minimum LMS: auth, checkout, entitlement, text module, email | Test payment grants correct module once; refund/retry cases handled |
| 5 | Closed beta with 30–50 real learners, optional Community | Completion, support burden, paid interest, content fixes |
| 6 | Launch 3–6 modules, TikTok/SEO distribution, first small A/B tests | Actual conversion, CAC and second-purchase baseline |

Make the build milestone conditional on validated module demand, not a fixed promise that six weeks guarantees RM10k.
