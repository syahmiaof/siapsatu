## Positioning guardrails

- Do not publish **“platform pertama di Malaysia”** without a defensible, tightly defined evidence claim. Existing Malaysian services already teach AI in Bahasa Melayu.
- Avoid **“anda akan ketinggalan jika abaikan AI”** as the core headline. The chosen line creates urgency and agency without scolding learners.
- Avoid **“semua tools AI”**: the product will curate relevant tools; no platform can realistically cover every tool or keep them all current.
- Generated screenshots use imaginary example interfaces, not actual vendor UI or official logos. Source current vendor marks and product names separately before implementation.
