## Product notes

- `Tools` is a first-class navigation item, not a buried homepage section.
- Tool pages group multiple RM10 modules, creating a natural repeat-purchase loop.
- Learning paths package related modules at a small bundle discount without hiding the RM10 entry price.
- Progress is expressed as finished work: a workflow, landing page, ad campaign or deployed site.
- The lesson format stays within the confirmed product scope: text, images, official links, templates and practical tasks.
- The old `ui-04-learning-path-automation.png` and `ui-10-tools-directory-mobile.png` remain only as comparison drafts; use the `-v2` files for final review.
