## Repair order

1. **Fix tool dead ends.** Cards with zero published modules should offer a useful tool explainer/waitlist or be visibly non-navigable; clicking should never land on a generic empty search. Keep live module counts truthful.
2. **Match the `/tools` mobile first fold.** Align headline position, hero crop, search and filter spacing, card dimensions, logo scale and featured order with `mockups/ui-10-tools-directory-mobile-v2.png`. Do not copy the mockup's invented module counts.
3. **Repair the reader page.** Compare `/learn/n8n-google-sheets/2` at the reference viewport; restore the workflow illustration and reading hierarchy without introducing a sticky-footer overlap.
4. **Polish home and catalog art.** Recreate missing handwritten accents, flag/arrow shape, community artwork and card graphics with clean source assets or DOM/SVG. Avoid screenshot crops containing baked-in UI/text.
5. **Audit credibility and launch readiness.** Community interactions, sign-in and checkout are demos; ensure any counts, testimonials, payment affordances or WhatsApp invitation do not imply live service until backed by real data/integration.
