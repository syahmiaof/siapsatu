# Market research and evidence log

Checked 14 October 2026. This is a demand hypothesis backed by population, digital use and skills signals. It is **not** proof that 30 named modules will sell. Paid demand still needs a landing-page, waitlist or small-sale test.

## Research interpretation

The strongest first-market hypothesis is **owner/operators with a task due this week**, followed by **students with an assignment or portfolio project due soon**. These buyers have a concrete trigger. “Semua rakyat Malaysia” is a reach ambition, not useful launch targeting.

Prioritise modules by four factors, scored after actual tests: urgency of problem, clarity of finished result, ease of showing a 20-second demo, and next-module fit. Tool-specific modules such as Gemini Canvas or Antigravity can capture search interest, but their UI changes quickly and increases maintenance. Outcome names should lead the catalog; tool names belong in descriptions and search metadata.

## Price and platform constraints

- [Billplz published pricing](https://main.billplz.com/pricing): free Basic plan lists FPX B2C fee RM1.25 per transaction; RM999 annual plan lists RM0.75. Card and wallet fees differ. Verify integration and current plan before choosing a provider.
- [Stripe Malaysia standard pricing](https://stripe.com/en-my/pricing): 3% + RM1.00 for domestic card and FPX payments at time checked. On RM10, that is RM1.30, or 13% of price.
- [WhatsApp's own pricing/help](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1022626499062292/) shows marketing-message charging depends on delivered messages and recipient country. Do not treat promotional WhatsApp as free infrastructure.
- [WhatsApp Channels](https://faq.whatsapp.com/265055289421317/) are one-way and public; they are useful for broad updates but are not the paid support room. [Telegram's documented group bot rights](https://core.telegram.org/api/rights) make automation technically clearer there, but the founder chose WhatsApp Community for the first batch.
- [TikTok Direct Post API](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-posting-api-get-started) needs an approved `video.publish` scope and an audit before public visibility. Start with AI-assisted drafts and human review; public auto-posting is a later integration milestone.
- [Malaysia's data protection commissioner](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/data-subject-rights/) describes a person's right to refuse direct marketing. Separate purchase messages from marketing consent and honor opt-outs.

## Validation still needed

1. Interview 10 owners and 10 students about the last task they could not finish. Collect exact wording and what they already tried.
2. Publish three topic-specific waitlist or pre-sale pages, each with a sample page and measured clicks. Compare conversion by segment; avoid mixing both audiences in one ad.
3. Sell the first 3–6 finished modules to a small cohort, then measure completion, refund, support questions and 30-day second purchase.
4. Repeat quarterly; the demand ranking in [BUSINESS_STRATEGY.md](BUSINESS_STRATEGY.md) is editorial judgement until these numbers arrive.
