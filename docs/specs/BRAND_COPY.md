# SiapSatu — copy direction

Draft, 14 October 2026. Product name is favoured by the founder; domain, trademark and handles remain unchecked. Price is RM10 for one standalone module. The copy promises a finished outcome, not mastery of an entire tool from one short guide.

## Recommended landing hero

**Brand line:** SiapSatu — Satu modul. Satu hasil.

**Category cue:** Panduan mudah untuk AI, tools & software

**Creator line:** Syahmi · Cloud, AI & Automation Educator

Use the creator line as a small trust cue below the tagline or in the landing page trust strip. Keep it out of the compact favicon and small logo lockup so the mark remains readable.

**Headline:** Dari tak tahu, sampai siap.

**Supporting copy:** Ikut langkah bergambar, buka tool melalui link terus, dan cuba dengan contoh serta template siap guna. RM10 satu modul, dengan satu hasil yang boleh anda terus guna.

**Primary CTA:** Pilih modul pertama

**Secondary CTA:** Lihat contoh modul

**Trust microcopy near CTA:** Tengok isi dan hasil modul sebelum beli. Sebarang kos tool tambahan dinyatakan pada halaman modul.

The price is shown as a plain number rather than calling the product “murah”. This preserves a premium feel and gives buyers the exact information they need. “Mudah” is supported by the step-by-step format; “siap” must be earned by a clear output checklist in every module.

## Two hook alternatives for a first A/B test

Keep the same supporting copy, price, module cards and CTAs so the headline is the main changed variable.

| Variant | Headline | Hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — outcome (recommended) | Dari tak tahu, sampai siap. | Broad appeal to owners and students; reinforces SiapSatu. |
| B — friction | Banyak tutorial disimpan. Mana hasilnya? | Speaks to people who are stuck between learning and doing. |
| C — direct | Belajar AI, tools & software. Terus buat sendiri. | Maximum category clarity for cold traffic. |

Run owner and student traffic separately. Compare module-card click-through, sample-view rate, checkout-start rate, purchase conversion and revenue per qualified visitor; watch refund/support as a quality check. Early traffic can guide interviews, but do not call a winner from a handful of purchases.

## Short versions for placements

- **Navbar descriptor:** Panduan AI & kemahiran digital, RM10/modul
- **Social bio:** AI, tools & software tanpa pening. Satu modul RM10, satu hasil yang boleh terus dibuat. ↓ Pilih modul pertama
- **Meta description draft:** SiapSatu menawarkan modul praktikal RM10 untuk belajar AI, tools dan software. Ikut panduan bergambar, buka link rasmi, guna template dan siapkan satu hasil.
- **Module-card CTA:** Lihat hasil & isi modul
- **Checkout CTA:** Beli modul ini — RM10
- **After-completion CTA:** Pilih hasil seterusnya

Avoid “kuasai AI dalam 30 minit”, guaranteed income, guaranteed ad performance and fabricated learner counts. Display real founder credentials and learner examples only when verified and permitted.
