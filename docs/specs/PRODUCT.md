# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Malaysian non-technical adults and business owners who want practical AI and digital skills without jargon.
- Computer science / IT students at beginner and intermediate levels who need to make real websites, projects and useful work artifacts.

## Product Purpose

Sell affordable, standalone learning modules at RM10 each. A learner should finish a concrete result, then discover the next relevant module. The founder's business goal is RM10,000 monthly net income from module sales, subject to validation of demand and unit economics.

## Positioning

Short Malay-first, outcome-led modules built around text, images, direct links to the correct tools, templates, worked examples and a task to try. The product should answer “apa nak buat sekarang?” for someone who does not know what to learn next.

## Operating Context

- People discover a topic, create an account, pay online, and gain access to the purchased module automatically.
- They receive a purchase/access email and, when opted in and operationally available, WhatsApp communication.
- An optional WhatsApp Community supports members and future launches. The founder wants automation to handle routine support, information sharing and content promotion.
- A live first module exists at `https://modul-meta-ads.syahmiaof.my/` and serves as a content and UX reference.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- RM10 is the intended price for one standalone module. Bundles are an unapproved experiment, not the default product promise.
- Modules use text, images, direct external links, templates, workflows and examples. No course videos in the base format.
- Mobile reading, low jargon, and clear links matter for the primary audience.
- The proposed account, entitlement, email, WhatsApp and community automation are planned capabilities, not implemented features.
- A live class may be considered later but is outside the initial self-serve model.
- Product name, exact stack, payment provider, legal entity, brand assets and precise support service level remain undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing live Meta Ads / AI automation module linked above. It has a chapter menu, terminology explanations, tool links, examples and checklist. A read-only audit is recorded in [UI_PLAN.md](UI_PLAN.md).
- There are no validated sales, conversion, completion, repeat purchase, customer acquisition or testimonial numbers in this workspace.

## Product Principles

1. One module should promise one demonstrable result and state prerequisites, time, cost of third-party tools and difficulty honestly.
2. Teach the next action with screenshots and direct links, then give an editable template and a small task.
3. Keep marketing, access rights and community membership separate so learners control promotional contact.
4. Use learning paths to recommend the next module from the learner's goal and completed work, not a generic upsell carousel.
